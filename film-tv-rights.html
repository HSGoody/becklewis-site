Rights file 01 • four novels and continuing

Dead Ends

A capable Leicester police team, pushed into institutional exile, investigates the cases nobody wants—each one placing an ordinary civilian at the psychological heart of a dangerous mystery.

Series engine

Self-contained investigations combine a recurring ensemble procedural with the intimate viewpoint of the civilian whose life has been overturned.

Screen identity

Contemporary Leicester, dry workplace humour, social pressure, moral complexity and three investigators who genuinely trust one another.

Opening case

Double Take: police shoot a man a witness says was armed. When no gun is found, the witness is publicly destroyed and the detectives must decide what truth costs.

Tonal territory

Slow Horses, Happy Valley and Broadchurch: regional, character-led and institutionally sharp.

Rights file 02 • female-led revenge thriller

Female Rage

When every respectable system protects the corporate heir whose abuse destroyed her friend, relentlessly pleasant trainer Nicole Varkonyi turns to her eighty-year-old former-spy grandmother for the skills to dismantle the boys’ club herself.

Story engine

Every target exposes a wider network of protected men, professional fixers, predatory technology and bystanders who have mistaken pleasantness for powerlessness.

Screen identity

Vivid femininity against corporate-noir darkness; black comedy, concealed weapons, a formidable grandmother and cathartic consequences.

Lead character

Nicole is an ordinary, funny, sociable woman learning lethal competence without surrendering the personality everybody underestimated.

Tonal territory

Sweetpea, Killing Eve and Promising Young Woman: dark wit, female agency and morally complicated revenge.

Rights file 03 • two novels and continuing

Dominion House

When a woman is found buried in Hagley Woods with her hands missing, a detective raised in a philosophical cult and a disgraced former policeman must identify her before another vulnerable woman disappears into the same hidden system.

Series engine

Methodical, evidence-led cases unfold through an affectionate but understaffed major-crimes team, with Ash Rigney’s unusual way of seeing reality at the centre.

Screen identity

Worcester, Hagley Woods, industrial estates and abandoned spaces create a distinctive landscape of Midlands procedural noir.

Returning pairing

DC Ash Rigney is a metaphysical outsider; civilian investigator Caleb Barrett is a former detective whose disgrace cannot be neatly undone.

Tonal territory

Happy Valley, Line of Duty and True Detective: social realism, procedural precision and philosophical unease.
